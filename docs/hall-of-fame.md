## The inductees

### 🥇 [#4191 · Preview Stutter that appears in Streams/Recordings](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/4191)

![comments: 184](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-184-blue?style=flat-square) ![locked: off-topic](https://img.shields.io/badge/locked-off--topic-red?style=flat-square)

> Support staff replicated the stutter in Feb 2021. The issue is still open, but it has been locked since Jan 2022. Before the lock, off-topic comments were deleted, a reporter was told they had been "unnecessarily combative", the cause was put down as "likely coming from outside OBS", and a link to a test build that users said fixed it was removed as "shady file hosting".

### 🥈 [#3555 · OBS Virtual Camera may affect built-in webcams](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/3555)

![comments: 87](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-87-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ![locked: off-topic](https://img.shields.io/badge/locked-off--topic-red?style=flat-square) ![stale sweep: 2024-10-14](https://img.shields.io/badge/stale_sweep-2024--10--14-orange?style=flat-square)

> Users reported their laptops' built-in webcams disappearing after using the OBS virtual camera. A staff member noticed the affected cameras shared one Sunplus OEM model. In Dec 2022 the thread was locked with "next steps is us purchasing a machine with one of these affected cameras". In Oct 2024 it was closed as stale and called "a hardware or kernel bug and not something we can realistically fix", because "nobody reporting the issue has been able to give us access to hardware".

### 🥉 [#12650 · Removal of --disable-shutdown-check breaks automated background workflows](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/12650)

![comments: 64](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-64-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ![locked: resolved](https://img.shields.io/badge/locked-resolved-red?style=flat-square)

> OBS 32.0 removed --disable-shutdown-check, which unattended setups used to skip the unclean-shutdown prompt. Staff told users to fix whatever was crashing OBS and said "that decision has already been made". They did point to PR #12668 for spurious prompts. Then they locked the thread as "resolved", although nothing had been resolved.

### 🏆 [#2949 · Dock Sizes are Reset the next time you open OBS.](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/2949)

![comments: 34](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-34-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ![stale sweep: 2024-10-14](https://img.shields.io/badge/stale_sweep-2024--10--14-orange?style=flat-square)

> Staff first said it was "100% reproducible for some people". A user later found it only happens when OBS is full screen. It was closed in the Oct 2024 sweep as "an issue with permissions and not a bug with OBS".

### 🏆 [#3148 · Pause and Resume Recording Doubles or Triples CPU usage](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/3148)

![comments: 34](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-34-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ![stale sweep: 2024-10-14](https://img.shields.io/badge/stale_sweep-2024--10--14-orange?style=flat-square)

> A staff member reproduced it in 2020, while others could not. Users still reported it on OBS 29 and 30. It was closed in the Oct 2024 sweep as "a catch-all for generic performance issues".

### 🏆 [#2724 · [BUG] OBS duplicates scenes and corrupts scene collections when using groups](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/2724)

![comments: 30](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-30-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square)

> Scene collections were corrupted when using groups. A staff member traced the corruption to OBS commit c0e2e7f (drag-and-drop reordering), and another said "It is a priority". Two years later it was closed with "most of the evidence here suggests" an old third-party plugin was to blame.

### 🏆 [#3060 · "Enforce Streaming Service Encoder Settings" Checkbox disables Rate Control setting](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/3060)

![comments: 26](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-26-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ![stale sweep: 2024-10-14](https://img.shields.io/badge/stale_sweep-2024--10--14-orange?style=flat-square)

> "Enforce streaming service encoder settings" silently forces CBR. In 2022 staff said "the pain point is felt on our end as well" and that they were looking into solutions. In the Oct 2024 sweep it was closed as "not a bug and working as intended".

### 🏆 [#13685 · Version 32.2 broke NVENC encoder for legacy graphics cards](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/13685)

![comments: 18](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-18-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square)

> 32.2 moved the NVIDIA SDK update that had been planned for 33 into a point release, which raised the minimum driver to 570. Staff first said "we did not drop any support for older GPUs", corrected that in the same comment, added "if you refuse to follow the process, there is nothing we can do", and closed the issue as "nothing actionable" the next day. A note about the minimum driver was added to the release notes afterwards.

### 🏆 [#2933 · [BUG] VLC Video Source > Add Path/URL to Playlist > displays incorrectly in a Scene](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/2933)

![comments: 18](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-18-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ![stale sweep: 2024-10-14](https://img.shields.io/badge/stale_sweep-2024--10--14-orange?style=flat-square)

> Staff confirmed it twice, on 26 RC1 and again on 28 ("it does look like this is still an issue"), and a user posted local repro steps. It was closed in the Oct 2024 sweep as stale, with a request to open a new issue.

### 🏆 [#3387 · Pasting a source does not show up in the sources until switching from different scenes](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/3387)

![comments: 16](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-16-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ![stale sweep: 2024-10-14](https://img.shields.io/badge/stale_sweep-2024--10--14-orange?style=flat-square)

> A staff member said "The issue is valid, but requires a fair bit of steps to repro", and users confirmed it on RC builds. Another staff member could not reproduce it on 28. It was closed in the Oct 2024 sweep as stale for "missing some key step".

### 🏆 [#9285 · Dropped frames on recordings in random moments, nothing which could cause it is happening](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/9285)

![comments: 15](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-15-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ![locked: too heated](https://img.shields.io/badge/locked-too_heated-red?style=flat-square)

> The reporter said rolling back to 29.0.2 removed the dropped frames. Staff said the samples were identical and that this is "just how compositing works". When users pushed back, the reply was "No amount of 'I don't like your answer' will change it", and the thread was locked as too heated.

### 🏆 [#11478 · [Flatpak] Runtime org.kde.Platform branch 6.6 is end-of-life](https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio/issues/11478)

![comments: 12](https://img.shields.io/badge/comments-12-blue?style=flat-square) ![closed: not planned](https://img.shields.io/badge/closed-not_planned-lightgrey?style=flat-square) ![locked: off-topic](https://img.shields.io/badge/locked-off--topic-red?style=flat-square)

> Flatpak OBS kept running on an end-of-life KDE runtime. Staff closed the issue as not planned while saying the update would come with a later release, called the EOL notice "just an overly dramatic warning in a package manager", answered a user who cited Qt security fixes with "We will update when we've verified the packages don't cause regressions", and locked the thread.

## How an issue gets in

1. `tools/upstream_hall_of_fame.py` searches `obsproject/obs-studio` for
   issues closed as *not planned* or locked, sorted by comments and reactions.
2. It keeps those with at least 15 comments or 8 reactions.
3. A person reads every remaining thread. Issues traced to a third-party
   driver, plugin or distro are thrown out. The verdicts live in
   `tools/upstream_hall_of_fame_verdicts.json`.

Every claim links to the thread. Read it and judge for yourself.

**Hit one of these? [OBS-Studio-Rust](../README.md) is where we fix them.**
